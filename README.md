# Spirited Away 

> *"Once you've met someone, you never really forget them. It just takes a while for your memory to return."*

**Spirited Away** (千と千尋の神隠し) is a 2001 animated fantasy film written and directed by **Hayao Miyazaki** and produced by *Studio Ghibli*.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Release Year | 2001 |
| Director | Hayao Miyazaki |
| Studio | Studio Ghibli |
| Runtime | 125 minutes |
| Notable Award | Academy Award for Best Animated Feature |

## Plot Summary

Ten-year-old **Chihiro** wanders into a mysterious spirit world while moving to a new town with her parents. After her parents are transformed into pigs, she must work in a bathhouse run by the witch **Yubaba** to free them and return home.

## Main Characters

- **Chihiro (Sen)** – the brave and resourceful heroine
- **Haku** – a mysterious boy who helps her
  - Secretly a river spirit
- **No-Face (Kaonashi)** – a lonely, shadowy spirit
- **Yubaba** – the bathhouse's powerful witch
- **Kamaji** – the six-armed boiler man

## Why I Love It

1. **Stunning hand-drawn animation** that feels alive
2. **Rich themes** of growing up, greed, and identity
3. **Joe Hisaishi's score**, which is unforgettable
4. A world full of **imagination and wonder**
